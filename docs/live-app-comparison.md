## Initial Positioning

Zomato is an adjacent comparison, not a direct mess-management competitor: its public site presents a consumer food-discovery and ordering service. MessMate is intended to coordinate a closed institutional meal workflow across students, administrators, and kitchen staff, including opt-ins, attendance, billing, forecasts, and waste records.

Do not describe a feature as absent from MessKeeper or HostelDekho based on this table. Their pages could not be extracted during this desk research, so their product scope and individual capabilities remain unverified.
